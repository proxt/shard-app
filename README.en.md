## What is Shard

Shard is a place where your people gather: friends, your gaming squad, your community. Hop into a voice lobby, chat in channels and direct messages, call and share your screen — all in one lightweight app.

We are building Shard because we want a simple and fast way to stay in touch with the people we care about, without the noise.

## Who we are

Shard is made by **a team of three developers**. We build the app, the server and the website ourselves, ship the updates and read every piece of feedback.

## What's there already

- **Lobbies** — a space for your group: text and voice channels, roles, invites.
- **Voice channels** — join and talk, with noise suppression and push-to-talk.
- **Screen sharing** — show your game or desktop right in voice.
- **Direct messages and calls** — one-on-one chats and calls with friends.
- **Friends** — add people and see who is online.
- **Articles feed** — lobby news and Shard updates.
- **Levels and achievements** — for you and for your lobby, with tasks you complete together.
- **Market** — profile customisation for Shards.
- **Account protection** — two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app or email.

The app is in Russian for now.

## Found a bug or have an idea?

Every piece of feedback matters, especially now, in alpha.

- **Bug** — [open a «Bug» issue](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml): what you did, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.
- **Idea** — [open an «Idea» issue](../../issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- There is also a **«Сообщить об ошибке»** (Report a bug) button in the app.

You can write in Russian or English. Please don't post passwords, verification codes or other personal data in issues.
